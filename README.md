# Who-is-driving-for-who

Favor, Door Dash, Uber Eats are delivery apps that provides driver delivery of food, groceries, or whatever someone might need from any store. The app sends offers to drivers stating what the delivery will pay, how many miles it will take from where the driver is, to the store, to the delivery point, and what the item/items are to be delivered. The driver then can make a decision weather the offer is worth the money and time to accept, or decline and wait for other offers. The problem I plan to solve is to help delivery drivers keep track of the gas they spend for tax breaks and estimate which offers are best to accept for a most profitable workday. I will create a program that will document information from the driver on a daily basis. The program will also do a few calculations for the driver. The program will ask for inputs like, enter name, miles per gallon your vehicle gets, price per gallon of last fill up. If the driver is already existing The program will show a display that will offer a list of commands, enter a day of work data, list of fill ups and total of list, sum earned from days of work. The program will store all the information on a csv file, and each day will be time stamped. The program will output information that the driver requests. The program will output, total earned, total earned minus price per mile of miles driven, dollars per hour of total earned minus price per mile of miles driven, list of fill ups and total of list, sum earned from and to dates of total earned minus price per mile of miles driven. The program will help delivery drivers keep track of gas spent for tax breaks and help drivers estimate what offers are best to accept on a work shift.
